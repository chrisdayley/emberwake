The Emberwake orchestral suites

Music by Scott Buckley — released under CC BY 4.0.

Each expedition has a distinct continuous 35-minute arrangement of licensed orchestral recordings. These are curated suites, not newly commissioned compositions. Recordings have been excerpted, loudness balanced, crossfaded and encoded to AAC for mobile playback. No recording is repeated within a suite. Some movements are shared between expeditions.

The opening develops into more forceful orchestration at 10 and 20 minutes, with a final battle sequence beginning at 30 minutes. Reward menus keep the music moving; manual pause and backgrounding preserve its position. At 35 minutes, the suite finishes naturally without looping. Combat and its sound effects can continue.

Music streams as needed. An internet connection is required for recordings that have not been downloaded by the browser.
