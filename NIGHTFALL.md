# Nightfall update

Your expedition changes at **10:00**, and continues for as long as you can survive. Thirty-minute dawn remains an optional milestone.

## Regions unlock sooner

- Survive **10:00 in The Ashwood** to unlock Violet Hollow.
- Survive **10:00 in Violet Hollow** to unlock Cinder Wastes.
- Unlocks are saved immediately. You can finish and keep your gold, or continue fighting.
- Existing ten-minute best runs unlock Hollow. Previously unlocked regions stay unlocked; old saved expeditions retain their time, loadout, equipment, and currency.

## Builds keep growing

Weapons still stop at rank **8**, and supporting skills at rank **3**. At ten minutes, level-up choices include Ascension upgrades. If you fill and max your build earlier, all three choices become Ascensions immediately. They use no slots and last for the current expedition.

| Ascension | Each choice grants |
| --- | --- |
| Unbound flame | +8% damage multiplier across the build |
| Heart of the wild | +15 maximum health and heal 15 |
| Ancient aegis | 1 additional armor and heal 10; up to 20 ranks |
| Wayfarer's stride | +2% movement speed, up to +40%; +12 pickup radius continues each rank |
| Boundless horizon | +4% area, up to +80%; +8% knockback continues each rank |
| Undying ember | +0.15 health per second |
| Accelerant | 2% faster spell recharge per rank, up to 15 ranks; total recharge cannot fall below 30% of base |

Damage, health, regeneration, pickup, and knockback tracks support 10,000 ranks each. A finished build also receives Ascensions from treasure chests instead of converting every upgrade into gold.

## New dangers and rewards

Dread bulwarks arrive at minute 10 with heavy armor and high health. Void stalkers at minute 12 are fast hunters. Siege colossi at minute 14 mark delayed ground blasts. Regenerating dusk harbingers arrive at minute 18. Their share of the horde increases gradually during their first minute. Enemy health keeps scaling faster after ten minutes; the regular spawn curve stays continuous, including the previous smooth ramp at minutes three and four. The five-ranged-enemy limit remains.

The first **Overlord** becomes eligible at 10:00. Only one living guardian or Overlord is scheduled at a time after that point; defeating it permits the next scheduled appearance. Overlords start at 95,000 health (118,750 in Cinder), scale with run time, and attack more often with more ground warnings below half health. Warnings give at least 1.6 seconds to move. Their chests contain **five upgrades, at least 550 gold before region bonuses, and guaranteed gear**.

After 10:00, hunting ground strikes periodically mark your position. Keep moving out of the warning circle.

## Shrine hunts

Three mint-colored shrines appear after ten minutes. Reach each one within 90 seconds. Mint compass arrows and distance labels guide you offscreen. Completing a hunt awards **180+ gold, 20 healing, and a bonus Ascension choice**. The next hunt starts 45 seconds after completion. If a hunt expires, another starts 30 seconds later; there is no failure damage. Timers pause with the game, and partial hunts survive save/resume.

## Three different battlefields

- **The Ashwood:** open forest with room to kite; hunting roots begin after ten minutes. The Rootbound Titan guards its late treasure.
- **Violet Hollow:** ruined courtyards with solid walls and open passages. Rift fields warn before activating, then damage and slow characters standing in them. Enemies move 15% faster; gold is worth 25% more. Its Overlord is the Rift Matriarch.
- **Cinder Wastes:** glowing lava channels divide basalt lanes. Stone bridges provide safe crossings; lava damages and slows you. Enemies have 25% more health; gold is worth 50% more. Face the Furnace Tyrant and its wider ground blasts.
